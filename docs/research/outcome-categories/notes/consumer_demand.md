## KQ1. Which 3D-printed product types dominate on Etsy, Amazon (incl. Handmade), eBay and Shopify stores?

### Takeaway
Across Etsy, Amazon and eBay the visible 3D-printed bestsellers cluster into (a) fidget/articulated toys (dragons, flexi animals, clicker keychains), (b) small functional "everyday" objects (bag clips, desk/cable/phone accessories, kitchen gadgets, planters), (c) personalized items (name keychains, name pencil toppers, pendants, lithophanes, pet tags, nameplates) and (d) gaming/tabletop accessories; trend articles consistently say the winning angle is personalization ("the custom angle buyers can't get at a mall").

### Cited Findings
- Etsy scale context: as of 31 Dec 2024 Etsy had >100 million active listings, 8 million sellers and 96 million buyers — [Wikipedia: Etsy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Etsy)
- The #1 item in EtsyHunt's "Top 100 best selling 3d printed on Etsy 2025" ranking is the "Funny Cat Butt Bread Clip: 3D Printed Reusable Bag Sealer" (a novelty functional kitchen item) — [EtsyHunt](https://ehunt.ai/best-etsy-3d-printed)

### Inferences
- The single most repeated demand pattern is "cheap functional novelty + personalization" (bag clip, name keychain, name pencil topper, pet tag, nameplate). These are exactly "integrative carriers": a user idea can ride on them.
- Fidget/articulated toys dominate raw unit volume on Amazon/eBay but are commoditized (identical STL bundles, "everyone sells dragons"), so they are a weak differentiator unless personalized (e.g., a custom creature).
- Desk/tech and gaming accessories appear in every seller guide but rarely as "gifts"; they are self-purchase/utility carriers (phone stand, headphone stand, controller stand, keycap).
- The Etsy bestseller mix includes many digital STL bundles, signalling that a large share of "3D printed" Etsy demand is maker-to-maker, not consumer end-products; consumer end-product demand concentrates in personalized/gift SKUs.

- Keychain-scale, single-color, print-in-place objects dominate what people physically make — evidence for keychains/charms and small fidgets as the lowest-friction carriers.

## KQ3. Top gift occasions and use cases driving personalized 3D-printed purchases

### Takeaway
Birthdays are the largest single occasion for personalized-gift orders (36% in one vendor's 2026 dataset), followed by anniversaries and memorial gifts; Christmas is the largest spend event overall and "personalized Christmas gift" searches spike late Nov–early Dec; Mother's Day, weddings, pets, fandom/gaming and (in India) Diwali/Rakhi plus corporate gifting are the other recurring drivers.

### Cited Findings
- Occasion share of personalized-gift orders (single-vendor data, 2026): birthdays 36%, anniversaries 21%, memorial gifts 18%, weddings 9%, holiday season 8%, graduations 5%, other 3% — [Beyond Memories, State of Personalized Gifting 2026](https://beyond-memories.com/blogs/beyond-memories/state-of-personalized-gifting-2026) (note: a memorial-gift vendor, so memorial share is likely over-represented)
- US spend by occasion (2025/26 compilations): Christmas/holiday ≈$932 per person (largest); Mother's Day ≈$254 (third-highest); average wedding gift $120 (close family $200+); birthdays $50–$75 per gift — [Elle Cadeaux, 40+ Gift-Giving Statistics 2026](https://ellecadeaux.com/blogs/fragrance-ideas/40-gift-giving-statistics-for-2026-how-much-americans-spend-what-they-buy-why); [NCH Stats](https://nchstats.com/americans-spend-on-gifts/)
- Etsy seasonality: Mother's Day demand (Feb–May) for personalized gifts, handmade and birthstone jewelry — [eRank, Mother's Day Gift Trends 2026](https://help.erank.com/blog/etsy-mothers-day-gift-trends-2026/); "personalized Christmas gift" searches "explode in late November through early December" — [Printify, top Etsy searches](https://printify.com/blog/top-etsy-searches/)

### Inferences
- For an app, the calendar peaks to build around are: Christmas (Nov–Dec, highest spend), Valentine's (Feb), Mother's/Father's Day (May/June), Diwali & Rakhi (Aug–Nov, India), with birthdays/anniversaries as the year-round base load (>50% of personalized orders in the one dataset found).
- Memorial/pet-memorial is an under-discussed but sizeable personalized niche (18% of one vendor's orders) that maps naturally to lithophanes, plaques and pet figurines.
- Kids/teachers/party favors favour sub-$10 carriers (name keychains, pencil toppers, bag tags); couples/memorial favour $30–$300 carriers (lithophane lamps, figurines).

- No multi-vendor or survey-based occasion split for personalized gifts (only one vendor dataset); no India-specific occasion shares.

## KQ6. Integrative vs decorative; trending vs saturated; consolidated ranking and under-served categories

### Takeaway
Integrative carriers (idea + useful object) with the strongest demand evidence are keychains/bag charms, desk/phone/headphone stands and cable/organizer gadgets, lithophane lamps/night lights, pet tags, nameplates/signs and keycaps; decorative categories with strong demand are fidget/articulated toys (saturated) and figurines (premium, growing via AI photo-to-3D).
